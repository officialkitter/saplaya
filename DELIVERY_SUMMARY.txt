═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                      HOW USERS INTERACT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

AGENT (Delivery Partner):
  1. Opens app, sees available orders nearby
  2. Taps "Claim Order" → Order assigned to agent
  3. Navigates to pickup location
  4. Marks "Picked Up" → Customer notified
  5. Navigates to delivery location
  6. Takes proof photo → Marks "Delivered"
  7. Commission auto-calculated (10% of order)
  8. Can request withdrawal to bank account

CUSTOMER A (Ordering for Someone Else):
  1. Selects "Order for Others"
  2. Fills recipient details (Customer B)
  3. Adds items, delivery address
  4. Places order
  5. Customer B receives email notification
  6. Available agents claim the order
  7. Same delivery process as regular order

ADMIN:
  1. Approves commissions
  2. Processes withdrawals
  3. Manages users
  4. Views analytics
  5. Manages platform settings
